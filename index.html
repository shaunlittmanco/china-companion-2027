Privacy & limitations

Imported bookings are saved in localStorage on your device, not encrypted. Avoid shared devices. Safari clearing website data or switching browsers will remove saved data. Export or re-import a backup.

Maps need internet, and mainland China may block some third-party tiles. Saved itinerary, phrasebook, currency math and imported bookings can work offline after first successful install.

Tracker sync uses only the read-only Google Sheets permission. Google describes this scope as viewing all your spreadsheets; this app requests only the fixed tracker. Access tokens stay in memory and disappear on reload. No client secret or refresh token is used. Two-way voice mode is experimental and can stop on iPhone Safari. Full reliable always-listening translation and live metro routing are not implemented.
