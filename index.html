You can open the included local files on a computer for inspection, but offline install/service worker requires HTTPS or localhost. GitHub Pages hosting is free for public repositories; keep the private JSON out of your repository.

Privacy & limitations

Imported bookings are saved in localStorage on your device, not encrypted. Avoid shared devices. Safari clearing website data or switching browsers will remove saved data. Export or re-import a backup.

Maps need internet, and mainland China may block some third-party tiles. Saved itinerary, phrasebook, currency math and imported bookings can work offline after first successful install.

Tracker sync uses only the read-only Google Sheets permission. Google describes this scope as viewing all your spreadsheets; this app reads only your privately configured tracker. Packing Sync separately uses file-scoped drive.file consent/Picker selection; writes are restricted by code to Packing & Bags. Access tokens stay in memory and disappear on reload. No client secret or refresh token is used. Two-way voice mode is experimental and can stop on iPhone Safari. Full reliable always-listening translation and live metro routing are not implemented.
